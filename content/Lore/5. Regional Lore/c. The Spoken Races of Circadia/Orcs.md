Orcs trace their creation to Shuraath, a powerful Great Spirit who roamed the wide open spaces of Circadia. Shuraath equipped his children with gifts to help them wander great plains, vast caverns, and churning seas and to face the monsters that lurk there. Even when they turn their devotion to another deity or God, orcs retain Shuraath’s gifts: endurance, determination, and the ability to see in darkness.

Orcs are, on average, tall and broad. They have gray skin, ears that are sharply pointed, and prominent lower canines that resemble small tusks. Orc youths on some worlds are told about their ancestors’ great travels and travails. Inspired by those tales, many of those orcs wonder when Shuraath will call on them to match the heroic deeds of old and if they will prove worthy of his favour. Other orcs are happy to leave old tales in the past and find their own way.

**The Scorching** is the name given to the final battle of the war between Lucidia and Octurna. Taking place in Western Lucidia, battle left the land now known as [[The Barrowlands]] scarred and largely uninhabitable. When Octurnan forces invaded from the southern coast through the [[Corpus Divide]], Lucidian forces met them and battle commenced. During the conflict, the then-reigning [[Hearth King Artoris Soleiran]] was killed. His son [[Hearth King Lugh Soleiran|Lugh Soleiran]] would ascend to the throne following the conflict, and a ceasefire would shortly be enacted between Lucidia and Octurna, bringing the war to a close.

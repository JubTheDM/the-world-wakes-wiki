The **Conquest of Illumination** was a long, vicious pilgrimage across the lands to liberate the people of [[Circadia]] of the Nightmares left in the wake of [[Remia]]'s [[Second Walking]]. The conquest was undertaken by a growing band of brave heroes, lead by one the people dubbed "[[The Hearth Knight]]" [[Finbarre Soleiran]].

## The Conquest
When Remia departed the lands for a second time, a dark presence would find a foothold in the land. Forces of shadow known as "[[Nightmares]]" began to pour forth across Circadia, laying waste to the settled civilisations as they were known in this period. The spoken races of the land would come together to try and survive, but few would make it their mission to fight back. One of the first to rise to the challenge was a gifted swordsman and surviving Knight of [[Oneira]] called Finbarre Soleiran. After losing almost everything during the previous war, he would dedicate himself to keeping the spark of life from extinguishing in Circadia.

The Conquest began as the exploits of Finbarre and a rag-tag band of fighters, which eventually became a capable party, which eventually became his true friends and retinue. The group continued to travel, vanquishing Nightmarespawn far and wide, inspiring the people and sharing a seat by the fire with them in celebration. This became customary, a custom that earned Finbarre the appellation "The Hearth Knight".

A number of years after the conquest began, Finbarre and his band would find themselves in the [[Southern Lands of Magic]]. This was a land where magic was plentiful, and stories of powerful sorceress by the name of [[Rhia Menebrae]] motivated Finbarre to parle and request aid. After proving himself to the Rhia and her people, and with the promise that when this was over the Southern Lands remain independent of the north, she agreed to join his band and lend their arcane prowess to their conquest. 

As Finbarre had hoped, this was made exponentially easier thanks to the help of Rhia and her faithful retinue. Together, the now hundred-person strong band of warriors spent the next two years venturing across the lands vanquishing Nightmares and liberating the peoples, until finally, the last Nightmare was slain. With this final act, the Conquest of Illumination had come to an end.

## Aftermath
In the weeks following the final confrontation, the grand force of good under Finbarre's leadership would disband. All involved returned to their homelands with stories that would be passed down for generations to come, least of all being the Hearth Knight himself. He would return to his home, and as a new era dawned on the land, the people longed for someone to lead them to face the coming sun's light together. And so [[The Kingdom of Lucidia]] would be founded in the north, with Finbarre Soleiran, now [[Hearth King]], at it's forefront.

Meanwhile, in the south, the nation of magic would remain it's own country as promised. [[The Lunar Court of Octurna]] would be founded with Rhia Menebrae on it's throne. With this final act, the [[Historical Eras of Circadia#The Era of Darkness|The Era of Darkness]] would come to an end in Circadia, and a new [[Historical Eras of Circadia#The Era of Illumination|Era of Illumination]] would begin.

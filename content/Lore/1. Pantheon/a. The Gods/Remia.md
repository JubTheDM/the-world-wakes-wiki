Remia is a god of the Circadian pantheon. She is the Goddess of Spirit, one of the three fundamental forces of the world. 

## Description
Like her fellow Gods, Remia resides in a higher plane beyond the comprehension of mortals. Unlike her dreaming brethren however, Remia is able to enter the world of Circadia through Lucid Dreaming, and walk among the mortals she helped to cultivate. When described by witnesses during her time in the realm, she was said to have long white hair, and resplendent eyes.

## Fractures
Remia has undergone a fundamental "Fracture" of her being twice, once during the [[Historical Eras of Circadia#The Era of Spirit|Era of Spirit]], and a second time during the [[Historical Eras of Circadia#The Era of Lucidity|Era of Lucidity]]. The first fracture was a result of suppression as the fundamental forces of light and dark smothered her influence, leading to a breaking point and the introduction of the forces of thought, hope, and longing being born into the world. This first fracture gave Remia the ability to lucid dream. The second fracture was as a result of immense grief, and in an attempt to partition this feeling she accidentally unleashed it on the world marking the beginning of the [[Historical Eras of Circadia#The Era of Darkness|Era of Darkness]].

## Walkings
### The First Walking
During the Era of Spirit, Remia would set foot on Circadian soil by way of lucid dreaming. This would mark the first time a God would set foot in the world of their creation. During her time in Circadia, Remia would bless a number of beasts with her power, creating the first [[Great Spirits]].

### The Second Walking
Unlike the first brief venture into the world, the Second Walking would play a pivotal role in the events of the Era of Lucidity. Remia, under the moniker "The Sage in White", would integrate herself into the civilisations of Circadia in an attempt to understand their desire for conflict. During this time, she would meet and fall in love with the leader of the Order of Oneira. Over time, the order would found the Kingdom of Oneira with the two reigning as monarchs, alongside their newborn child. 

Later, a war would claim the lives of Remia's husband and child. A level of grief that was foreign to her until now would consume her, and lead to her second fracture. Remia's grief was expelled into the world, giving birth to monstrous Nightmares that would go on to plague the land. Tired, Remia would withdraw once again.

## Worship
A religion dedicated to the worship and chronicling of Remia's exploits in Circadia would form during the time of her Second Walking, called [[The Path of Remia]]. This religion still persists to this day, although it has been surpassed by [[The Hallowed Hearth]] in terms of proliferation. Many relics from the Kingdom of Oneira are in The Path's possession, including many that were said to belong to Remia herself.

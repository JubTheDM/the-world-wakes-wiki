Ninota is a god of the Circadian pantheon. He is the God of Light, one of the three fundamental forces of the world. 

## Description
Often referred to as "The Maker", Ninota is worshipped as a force of absolute good. His depictions over time vary, but he is commonly depicted as the sun, or a bearded man with a crown of sunlight. Unlike his fellow Goddess Remia, Ninota has remained dormant since the beginning of time, however the fundamental force of Light that he embodies plays a pivotal role in the cosmology of Circadia.

## Worship
The primary religion of Ninota is The Hallowed Hearth, a theodicy based on the idea that his flame will protect them so long as one nurtures it within themselves. Many revere Ninota as a force of absolute good, and believe that the [[Conquest of Illumination]] to be of his will. Those that worship Ninota often believe Marean, Godess of the Dark, to be in stark opposition to their beliefs. Therefore worship of Marean is considered Taboo.

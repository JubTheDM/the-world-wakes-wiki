Marean is a god of the Circadian pantheon. She is the Goddess of the Dark, one of the three fundamental forces of the world. 

## Description
Often referred to as "The Maiden in Black", Marean is worshipped as the patron saint of magic, and while not all believe her to be inherently evil, she is often depicted as the inverse of Ninota. Her depictions over time vary, but she is commonly depicted as the moon, or a lady in a black dress clutching a silver disk. Unlike her fellow Goddess Remia, Marean has remained dormant since the beginning of time, however the fundamental force of Dark that she embodies plays a pivotal role in the cosmology of Circadia.

## Worship
Worship of Marean is considered taboo by those of the Hallowed Hearth faith, as to them, she represents the absence of light. The Sanctus Noctis are the primary religion, and revere Marean for blessing their lands with magic. Her moonlight is seen as sacred, and they believe her to be far from the force of ill most depict her as. 

The Marean Umbradoxy are a sect of the Sanctus Noctis that believe that the way to get closest to Marean is to plunge the world into darkness. Largely shunned by the larger Sanctus Noctis faithful, the Umbradoxy often works in the shadows to accomplish their extremist ideology.

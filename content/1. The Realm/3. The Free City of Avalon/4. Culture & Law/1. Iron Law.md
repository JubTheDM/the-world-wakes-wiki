Iron can only enter the realm of [[The Realm of Faeries|Faerie]] if brought there by mortals from the Material realm, as it occurs nowhere naturally save for upon the tough scales of Iron Wyrms. This makes iron the single most important resource in [[The Free City of Avalon|Avalon]], its extreme scarcity and powerful properties elevating it to a near-holy sacredness. As such, its possession is controlled tightly within the borders of the mortal lands, enforced by strict law. All who enter the bounds of the city must declare any iron or steel on their person, which is then taken to be deposited within the vaults of the Bank of Bullion before being worked into [[The Iron Price#Coldiron|coldiron]] by the smiths of the Iron Altar. Little is left to chance either, with passage into the city closely monitored by knights and mages practiced in the arts of divination. To hide or not declare iron is a crime punishable by death or imprisonment within Avalon. 

Only the smiths of [[1. The Brotherhood of Iron|the Iron Brotherhood]] are permitted to work iron, and only in the main forge within the Iron Altar. Once worked into coldiron, only certain knights of Avalon are then given permission to wield or wear it, such as [[7. The Knights Peregrine|the Knights Peregrine]], [[3. The Stone Guard|the Stone Guard]], or some [[5. The Bastion Brethren|Bastion Brethren]]. This permission is given in the form of a seal that the knights carry at all times, marked by the Grand Master of Iron upon the bestowing of their coldiron armament. 

The collection of iron is bolstered by the activities of the Knights Peregrine, who spend much of their time scouting the wider realm of Faerie in search of lost mortals or retrieving remnants left by those who perished whilst carrying such items. They regularly come into conflict with agents of [[The Cardinal Kingdoms|the cardinal kingdoms]], who work desperately to prevent Avalon from acquiring iron.

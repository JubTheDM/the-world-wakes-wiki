**Headquarters:** *The Bank of Bullion*
**Title of Leader:** *Lord Treasurer*

Bullion Knights are under the command of the Lord Treasurer, and are tasked with overseeing the Armourial of the grand Avalonian orders protection and storage of all wealth within the city. Perhaps their most sacred duty is the storage and protection of any iron delivered unto them by the Knights Peregrine, for use at the Iron Altar.

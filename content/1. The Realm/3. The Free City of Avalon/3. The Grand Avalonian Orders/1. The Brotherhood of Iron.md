**Headquarters:** *The Iron Altar*
**Title of Leader:** *Grand Master of Iron*

The Brotherhood of Iron might be the closest thing to a true religious order within the bounds of the free city. Its members are dubbed smiths, and are tasked with the sacred duty of working the city's reserve of iron into usable coldiron. This process is mystic, a closely guarded and hallowed practice entrusted to only the most devout. The position of Grand Master of Iron is currently held by the hand of the King, Weyland the Smith.

**Headquarters:** *The Tower of Emrys*
**Title of Leader:** *Grand Master of Emrys*

All practicing mages within the city limits of Avalon must make themselves known to the Order of Emrys, who sanction the use of magic within the city. Those of particular talent are even invited within its ranks, allowed to study what knowledge the order has managed to sequester over the ages past.
## Myrddin’s Men
Within the Order of Emrys there is a smaller and more secretive sect, called Myrddin's Men. This sect is named for the fabled wizard who travelled with the first Pendragon into Avalon and is tasked with learning and practicing the strange powers of elder magic so that the mortals of Avalon might better understand, and even wield, the weapons of their foes.

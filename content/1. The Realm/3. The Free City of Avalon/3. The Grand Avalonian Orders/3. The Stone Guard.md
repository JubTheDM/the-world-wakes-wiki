**Headquarters:** *Mortum Castle*
**Title of Leader:** *Lord Commander of Avalon*

The Stone Guard is the knightly order tasked with guarding the Pendragon and the Round Table, as well as entrusted with the safety of the city at large. They are not city guard, and do not enforce the laws of Avalon day to day, rather they protect the judicial systems of Avalon itself. The Stone Guard and its leader, hold the highest position of honour amongst the orders at the Round Table.

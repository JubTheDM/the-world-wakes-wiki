**Headquarters:** *The Iron Altar*
**Title of Leader:** *Knight Commander of the Peregrine*

Peregrine knights are in many ways a sect of the Brotherhood of Iron, save for the fact that they
maintain their own system of command outside of that of the Brotherhood. The duty of the Knights Peregrine is to venture beyond the walls of Avalon to seek mortal wanderers fresh to the realm that they can return safely to Avalon along with any iron they might carry.

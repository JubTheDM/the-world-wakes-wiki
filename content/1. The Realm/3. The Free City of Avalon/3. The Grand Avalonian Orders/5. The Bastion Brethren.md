**Headquarters:** *The Gateway Keeps*
**Title of Leader:** *Knight Commander of the Bastion*

The Bastion Brethren have guarded the walls of Avalon for an age, walking the palisades of wooden perimeters long before stone held back the tides of fey. Their time-honoured position as "Wall Knights" is so well known that they have come to be referred to by common folk and even other knights as "Walnuts", a name spoken in both jest and affection.

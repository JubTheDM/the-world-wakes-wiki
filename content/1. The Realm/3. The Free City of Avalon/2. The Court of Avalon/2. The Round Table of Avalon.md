Whilst the Pendragon holds rule in Avalon, he does not do so in isolation; each Pendragon is joined by the support of their council, eight knights who sit about the Round Table within Avalon's keep. These Knights each represent one of Avalon's eight grand orders, giving voice to the many sects that work to keep the city safe. Within the council there exists hierarchy further still, dictated by the power and importance of each represented order.

## The Avalon Hierarchy
**The Council of the Round Table:**
1. The Pendragon
2. Lord Commander of Avalon
3. Grand Master of Iron
4. Grand Master of Emrys
5. Lord Treasurer (money & trade)
6. Knight Commander of the Bastion
7. Knight Commander of the Watch
8. Knight Commander of the Peregrine
9. The Vassal Commander

[[The Free City of Avalon|Avalon]] is governed by a single monarch, who guides the people of Avalon, supported by the wisdom and expertise of a knightly council. This system of rule has been in place since the first days of Avalon, and has steered the city true through much strife and uncertainty.

The ruler of Avalon bears two marks of sovereignty in the form of a small [[The Iron Price#Coldiron|coldiron]] crown and the inherited title of [[the Pendragon|Pendragon]]. This title has been held by many, each having left their mark on the city's storied history and each having worn the humble iron crown. The crown itself is little more than a simple band of coldiron, forged in the earliest days of Avalon's history when iron was even more impossibly scarce than it is today. To have used even a scrap of it on a trinket such as this speaks to the power of its symbolism, as does the refusal of all that have worn it since to embellish or alter it despite its crude appearance.

Stretching out from the rear of [[1. Mortum Castle|Mortum Castle]] to meet the Watchtower of Avalon is a striking stone skyway dubbed the Bridge of Knells. The bridge's name was bestowed by Lord Commander Galahad of Avalon not long after its construction, from the suggestion that freshly dubbed knights would hear the celebratory ring of Avalon's bells as they walked from Mortum Castle after their completion of the knighting ceremonies held within.

Blazon Hall is perhaps the grandest structure to sit outside of [[The Free City of Avalon|Avalon]]'s outer walls, and the first many see on their way into the city. It is a fortified manor hall of sorts, built atop a hill that looks down across the main road into Avalon. Beyond its scale, the hall is easily identifiable by the many colourful banners of arms that billow from its battlements, each bearing the heraldry of a noble Vassal Knight.

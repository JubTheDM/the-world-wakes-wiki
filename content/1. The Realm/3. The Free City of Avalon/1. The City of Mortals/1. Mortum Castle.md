The grand keep of Avalon stands like a vast and vigilant monolith, a knight clad in stone armour keeping guard over the lands of men. Mortum Castle projects an impressive image upon the cityscape of [[The Free City of Avalon|Avalon]], yet its construction remains unadorned and humble. The simple sheer face of its stone walls combine at angled corners to form an imposing geometric silhouette. To those who might seek to strike at the heart of the free city, Mortum is a loud reminder of the city's strength and resilience. To those who seek shelter from the wilds of [[The Realm of Faeries|Faerie]], it is a beacon of sanctuary rising boldly from the verdant hills at its base. Stepping foot beyond its doors unveils a more grand interior, though still rudimentary in its construction, it is clear that its echoing halls and wide stone staircases are built to evoke a sense of regal tradition. Within these chambers, many busy themselves with the never-ending tasks that face those charged with running the city. Ancient tapestries telling tales of the city's inception, mythologies from before the years of Avalon, all play backdrop to the endless business that fills much of the castle's interior.

The castle's wall is punctured part way up its height by a large circular stained glass window that marks the chamber holding [[2. The Round Table of Avalon|the Round Table]]. The Table itself is somewhat crude in design, surrounded by nine stone seats, all of which are bathed in the colourful light that washes across the room from the window. It is within this chamber that the fate of Avalon is guided by [[the Pendragon]] and his council of knights.

The main gatehouses of [[The Free City of Avalon|Avalon]], one for each of Avalon's four borders with [[The Cardinal Kingdoms|the cardinal kingdoms]].
These stern structures are the most imposing of Avalon's outer defences, built to resist onslaught from land and sky, magic and mundane. Each holds a large oaken door within a grand stone barbican, protected when required by large portcullises made almost entirely of [[The Iron Price#Coldiron|coldiron]] (making them by far the largest, and thus most costly, coldiron creations in all of Avalon). It is manned at all times by knights of the Bastion Brethren, trained in operating the various defensive weaponry mounted atop its wall walk. They are even trained and assisted by mages of Emrys in the defence and use of simpler magics so as to be most prepared for any eventuality the fey might throw their way.

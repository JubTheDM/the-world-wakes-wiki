A lone tower stands proud in the shadow of [[1. Mortum Castle|Mortum Castle]], its spire escaping the muddle of rooftops below to point starward. This is the beating heart of all magic in Avalon, where the Order of Emrys sanction and practice the arcane arts. Because Avalon stands alone in a realm of mischief and chaos, care has always been taken to regulate the use of magic within the city. There are many malevolent beings who would seek to disrupt and destabilise Avalon, whose quest would be made far easier were magic left unchecked within the city's bounds. That isn't to say magic is banned, quite the contrary, but all those practicing the arcane are expected to make themselves known to the mages of Emrys. [[The Elder Magic|Elder magic]] is a different story, the mystical arts of witches and ancient legends, this is a power that only special few within Emrys are permitted to practice.

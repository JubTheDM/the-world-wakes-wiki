Deep within [[The Free City of Avalon|the city of Avalon]] stands a grand pillared building of soot-dark stone that emanates
a warm ember glow from its depths. It looks more like a temple than a forge, and in many ways it is; the Iron Altar is a place of both forge and of sacred duty, where the smiths of the brotherhood work sacred iron into mystical [[The Iron Price#Coldiron|coldiron]]. Many forges glow active within the bowels of the altar, working various metals and alloys into usable armour and weapons, but iron is worked only in a single forge at the building's centre. This main forge has not changed since its original construction, long before the grand temple that now houses it sprang up, remaining a humble forge in both size and design. In truth, so little iron moves through the forge that it is rarely put to use, yet it is always kept hot and well cared for.

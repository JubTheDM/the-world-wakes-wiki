>*Not all within Faerie fly the banner of fealty, though there are those who wander with common cause. Errants, devoid of lord or lady, joined in oath or community with others of similar nature. These are people who do not bow to any one king nor subscribe to the laws of any one land; they are sovereigns over their own fates. Be it the cloven hoof of the spectral Wildhunt or the nomadic tradition of the Wandering Hollow, these are forces that continue to play a role in the ever-changing landscape of the realm despite belonging to none of its greater powers.*

It is said that a strange thing wanders the elder places of Faerie; neither beast or spirit but a hamlet
of witches. Stories speak of huts that walk on two legs, wandering from place to place like a herd of beasts migrating across the realm, only settling for a time before once again standing to continue their endless trek. It is said that these enchanted huts belong to a coven of witches who travel across the lands of Faerie as a nomadic caravan of sorts, never staying in one place too long. Little is known of them, yet much is whispered of their myth and legend. Many have sought them out, seeking their aid or their blessing, but few have ever been able to find them.

#### Ancient Origins
The witches of the Wandering Hollow belong to a coven most ancient and largely forgotten. When
Blight sought to ruin the realm in ages past, the united forces of fey sought to harness the elder magic in defence of their lands and lives. Many tried and failed to coax this fickle power into something tangible to use against the hordes of House Blight, but none succeeded. In desperation, the Free Fey sought to combine their efforts, bringing together the most powerful witches from distant corners of the realm. These wise and ancient practitioners were hard-won, but eventually each discarded their neutrality in order to fight for the realm. A coven was formed, like none before it, and from that coven arose the creation of the menhir bronze. Metal weapons made of ancient bronze, engraved with intricate sigiladry and saturated with elder magic, capable of fighting back the hordes of decay. With these weapons, the Free Folk were able to push back the tide of rot, forming a perimeter around what remained of the realm. These weapons were then stored within the menhirs, buried in stone to form a boundary of elder magic that keeps the realm safe to this day. What became of the coven after this has been forgotten by most, but not all. Some who remember believe those witches remained bound by their bonds of coven, and that their descendants still wander the wilderness of the realm, maintaining a vigilant eye on the menhir ring crafted by their forebears.

#### Finding the Hollow
Fate and luck seem the only forces capable of guiding one to the Wandering Hollow, with legends speaking of its appearing to great heroes in times of most dire need. There are many who have tried in vain, even regents of the cardinal kingdoms have at times sought far and wide for the stalked legs of the Hollow's wandering huts. It seems that rather than finding the Hollow, fate decrees that instead the Hollow must find you.

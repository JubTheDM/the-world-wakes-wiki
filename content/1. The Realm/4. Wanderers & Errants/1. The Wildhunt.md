>*Not all within Faerie fly the banner of fealty, though there are those who wander with common cause. Errants, devoid of lord or lady, joined in oath or community with others of similar nature. These are people who do not bow to any one king nor subscribe to the laws of any one land; they are sovereigns over their own fates. Be it the cloven hoof of the spectral Wildhunt or the nomadic tradition of the Wandering Hollow, these are forces that continue to play a role in the ever-changing landscape of the realm despite belonging to none of its greater powers.*

The Wildhunt is a faction that knows loyalty to none of the Cardinal houses, more a force of nature than a collective to be reasoned with. It’s said that times of great turmoil within; the lands of Faerie will call the Wildhunt to ride, often seen during great conflicts, challenging and recruiting the greatest warriors, dead or alive. A ghostly battalion of mounted warriors sweeping across the battlefield is a sight that causes all but the bravest or most foolhardy of the Fey lords to turn and flee, but when it comes to the Hunt there are only three options: join, hide, or die. It’s unclear what the Hunt’s purpose is, whether they exist in some demi-plane or hidden place in the time between rides, or if they’re simply an ethereal force of chaos summoned into existence in times of war and destruction.

#### Roving Doom
None outside of the Hunt's rank really know how they are able to travel across the realm as they do. Some say they ride through the clouds themselves, their wraithish steeds stampeding on the bluster of a harrowing wind. Others say they walk between worlds, crossing the veil of the realm to pass indiscriminately between places, crossing miles as if in a single bound. Whatever the case may be, no one can be sure just where or when the Hunt may appear, and so all are vigilant to the spectral sound of their horn lest they fall prey to their eternal hunt.

#### Indiscriminate Recruiters
The Wildhunt welcomes, or presses all into their ranks regardless of lineage or creed. If you can fight, you’re a huntsman; if you can’t, you’re the prey. The forces of the Hunt are filled with some of the greatest warriors from Faerie's past, as well as those from beyond the realm. It’s even said that a great warrior can resist the Hunt’s influence for a period of time, taking advantage of the wild powers granted to them without fully succumbing to the bloodlust; however, in time, all join the ranks.

#### Head of the Pack
Throughout history, the Wildhunt has had countless leaders attributed to it. It became a mark of honour to claim that your society's greatest warrior or hero led the Hunt. It is claimed that even Autumn’s [[3. Herne the Hunter|Herne]] once rode alongside ghostly shucks claiming the power of the Hunt. Regardless of who was or could have been, the current Huntmaster is Dullahan, a fearsome warrior bearing blade and whip from atop his midnight-black war horse. Dullahan, oft referred to as the Headless Horseman, was in life was a fearsome fey warrior, utilising his whip crafted from the bones of his enemies and his Headtaker blade. He fell in a mighty battle with Herne himself, his head cleft from his shoulders, only to rise again as a more terrible and ghoulish captain than the hunt has ever seen.

The law of Feast is simple and resolute: when the bell tolls, the hungry shall eat. Better to be feaster
than the feasted upon, and if you cannot defend your meal, then it is yours no longer. As the adage goes, 'tis better to be a warrior in the kitchen than a cook in a war; nowhere is this more literal than the grounds of [[1. Gwledd Hollow|Gwledd Hollow]].

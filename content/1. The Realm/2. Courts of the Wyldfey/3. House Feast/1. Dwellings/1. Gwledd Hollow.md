>*House Feast are, as their namesake would suggest, goblinoid wyldfey of the most voracious spirit. Their deepest pleasure and desire is to consume whatever sustenance most deliciously presents itself, and they take great pride in their prestige as ancient gourmands.*

Rising from within the low mountains and misty moors of the northern [[3. Y Rhos Mawr|Y Rhos Mawr]] is the fortress of Gwledd, its large roundhouse keep surrounded by a wall of sharpened logs that form its paling perimeter. An unusual quiet is most often the state that falls upon Gwledd Hollow, a quiet that would give the impression it was little more than an old and forgotten ruin. This quiet is, however, merely the brief respite between havoc and feasting. Its end is called upon by the haunting toll of an ancient bell that echoes out from the hollow to call all who hunger to the feast within. Within the walls of the hollow lies a mess of bones and scraps, discarded peels, and empty shells. The bandings of long dry casks hang from branches and stonework, as if flung in celebration, and small goblinkin scurry about the place collecting bottles and mugs to be refilled. Once [[1. The Pumpkin King|the Pumpkin King]], ruler of Feast, has rung the great bell in summoning, the grounds of the Hollow swarm with life. Ovens churn out breads whilst vast baskets of fruit and root are greedily emptied. Roast meats and salt-cured jerky are torn from hooks and bones. Then, almost as quickly as it arose, this clamour dies down once again. Its participants, satiated for a time, fall back into lazy slumber and idle pursuits, slow and fat with gluttony.

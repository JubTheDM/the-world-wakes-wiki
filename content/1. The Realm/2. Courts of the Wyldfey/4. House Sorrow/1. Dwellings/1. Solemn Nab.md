>*House Sorrow is eternally besotted by endless melancholy. They are the embodiment of portent, cursed with visions of grim past and even grimmer future; they are forever wandering within the liminal scape of what was and what may yet be.*

The lone tower of Solemn Nab stands tall and singular from the peak of an unnamed mountain to
the south-eastern corner of Winter's lands. Within this tower is the wyldcourt of Sorrow, who live solely within its stacked chambers. At the tower's zenith there is a great font, around which sit thirteen tall stone chairs in which the court of Sorrow sit. From these seats, they watch the font, looking as visions dance upon its silver surface to portent doom and death. Many are drawn to Solemn Nab, called by the weeping of its council to join in their grief within the hallowed heights of the tower. It is said that from the font flows an endless waterfall, which cascades from its highest chamber down to its lowest and then out down the mountainside. Those who drink of this water are said to find a sad solace as visions of their grief or loss mingle with portents of their most dour futures. The tower itself is thus home to many, both those living in Sorrow, and apparitions brought forth by its magic.

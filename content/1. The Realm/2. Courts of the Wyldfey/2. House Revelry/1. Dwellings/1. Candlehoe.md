>*House Revelry is the manifestation of fanatic hedonism, passion in its rawest aspect, formed into thought and being. The fey of Revelry live deeply and relentlessly.*

Far to the north-east of Summer, upon a forgotten corner of its rugged coast, lies the glimmering glow of Candlehoe. From a distance, it seems to flicker, like the mirage of some great bonfire, until on approach its majesty is revealed. Tall towers capped in colourful stone spires rise behind pale stone walls draped in banners of gold and silver. Cradled within this nest of stone and colour is a pillared palace painted and gilded, decorated with billowing fabrics that dance with the firelight of great braziers that hang in eaves and archways. Even from afar, the sound of laughter and music echoes on, joined in chorus by smells of fine foods and ancient wines. The lands surrounding it are mottled with orchards of trees bearing vibrant fruit and marked with strange statues made from wicker. 

Candlehoe flows with the virility of Revelry, be it wine or blood. An endless festivity shakes the ground there, as merriment gives way to feast and then to fighting. Tournaments and competitions draw crowds who ebb and flow through the lands like a flock of birds performing murmuration.

>*House Burrow holds court in the regal hollow of Bower Hall, a grand ground dwelling built beneath and throughout a tall hillock crowned by an ancient oak. Beastfolk make up its people, anthropomorphic animals from whom all beasts were once said to descend.*

Unlike most courts, Bower Hall's majesty is hidden from view. Its regal purpose only evident in the
majestic bows of the ancient oak standing proud atop its grassy hillock. A single large door of living root marks its entrance, guarded at all times by furred warriors who watch from atop the limbs of the tree above. Beyond this door is a warren of sorts, only much grander. Living roots weave to create walls of patterned wood, polished river stones are laid across its chambers and hallways to create subtle mosaics, and incandescent insects nest in cultivated nooks to light passageways with a warm glow. At the heart of Bower Hall is its throne room, directly beneath the centre of the great oak. The oldest of the oak roots descend into the far side of the circular, domed chamber, knotting to form a throne of gnarled timber mottled with moss. It is from this seat that [[1. The Bear King|the Bear King]] rules over Burrow's domain, perhaps the heartiest embodiment of primal power to ever walk the realm.

The beastfolk of Burrow respect primal power more than anything; they believe that the struggle to
survive is the most ancient and sacred of pursuits, and those who physically embody such ideals are worthy of honour. This belief extends from the smallest insect to the mightiest beast, cultivating a culture that believes in empowering the weak to better fare in the eternal struggle against the cycle of death. Any who embody these ideologies, who embrace the primal or the animalistic, will be welcome in their lands.

The hillocks of the Hernewolds rise up from the meadows of the lower Egni in the west and continue eastward across Summer before they join the Vale of northern Autumn and ascend into the mountains. The rolling hills of the Hernewolds make for ideal livestock land, namely for the herding of sheep, and so shepherds and flocks are a common sight when travelling the Hernwolds of Summer. Unfortunately such abundance of livestock does bring with it the unwelcome ire of more voracious creatures; earning the wolds a reputation as the favoured hunting grounds of winged predators, its deceptively idyllic pastorals oft a stage to scenes most gruesome.

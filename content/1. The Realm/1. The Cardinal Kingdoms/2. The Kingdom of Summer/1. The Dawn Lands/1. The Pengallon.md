An ancient woodland crowds the perimeter of Spring’s westernmost borders, a natural wall of knotted timber that stands against the fringes of the Badlands. Brechelien is ancient, and holds many secrets. In fact many argue that Brechelien may be the oldest wood to exist within the safety of the Menhirs, its roots reaching deep into the well of the world itself to drink of water born from the first rain the grace the realm. Regardless of the veracity of these claims, none who dare venture beneath its shadowy bows would deny the palpable atmosphere that lingers in the air there. It has been described, by those fortunate few to have survived travel through Brechelien, that it feels as if the forest itself thinks and breathes with the world itself; And that in the quite of the darkest woodland there, you can hear the trees whispering to each other like clergymen hidden beneath unseen cloisters.

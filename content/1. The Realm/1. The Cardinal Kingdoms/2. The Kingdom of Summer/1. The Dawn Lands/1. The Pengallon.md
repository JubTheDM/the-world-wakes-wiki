The Pengallon, or Caladur Coast as it is more commonly known, defines the border of the
northernmost territories of [[1. House Summer|House Summer]]. This wide-reaching stretch of coast marks the only section of shoreline to fall within the province of any of the cardinal courts, and spans such a wide area that it encompasses a diverse range of differing geography and environment. The Sea of Mordu is the given name to the waters that lap at its cliffs and beaches, an ocean so mystic that it is said to reach beyond the veil and into the mortal realm itself. For House Summer it brings not bounty in fishing, maritime salvage, and raw material wealth; for the cliffs of the Pengallon hold perhaps the richest deposit of [[The Iron Price#Dur Steel|Dur]] in all the known lands of Faerie, keeping House Summer well armed and well armoured.

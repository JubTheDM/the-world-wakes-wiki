This dense woodland forms a wild and unruly natural boundary between the lands of Spring and Summer, with its northernmost expanse rooted firmly within the province of the latter. Crioch is, whilst still an ancient and well-rooted forest, thought of as younger than the woods of Brechelien. Some lore of the Crioch Frith even speaks of its first trees being grown from seedlings stolen from Brechelien itself by soldiers of House Summer during the later days of the last border war. The Egni cuts through the Frith en route to the Mordu from Spring, and is closely guarded by shields and blood of House Aberwell in loyal service of House Summer.

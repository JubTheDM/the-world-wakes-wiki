Sodden Hurst is a patch of woodland covering a large hillock that marks the south-easternmost lands of Summer, before they transition into the lands of Autumn. It gets its name from its proximity to Autumn, elevation, and dense woodland, all of which contribute to a weather pattern of increased rainfall. Despite the unflattering moniker, Sodden Hurst has proven to be a key strategic asset on numerous occasions in the history of House Summer, the ancient watchtower of Brigwyn was even built atop it in times long forgotten to serve that purpose

The lands of Summer are governed by three noble houses, each entrusted with a keep and its
surrounding lands:
## House Penrud 
Rule the northern coast of Summer from Brightcliff, granting them the title of "Lords of the Pengallon".
## House Aberwell 
Are the Riverlords of Crioch Frith, watching over Summer's Spring border from their seat at Avonry in the south-west.
## House Grailin 
Sits in the seat of Wollenbryn in the south, guarding the south-east of Summer and the Hernewolds.

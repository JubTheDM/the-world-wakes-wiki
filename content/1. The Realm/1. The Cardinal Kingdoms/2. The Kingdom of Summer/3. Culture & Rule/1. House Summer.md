Rule of House Summer falls to the [[1. Queen of Elphame|Queen of Elphame]], who above all others walks as the manifestation of her season. Her armour reflects distant sunlight even in the darkest of places, and her charm is so powerful that folk warn against gazing upon her too long, much as one warns against looking into the sun. Troubadours sing ballads of her ascension to the Throne of Summer; they say she was born little more than a lesser nobles daughter, yet she was gifted the most beautiful singing voice to ever grace the realm. Birds would often refuse to sing in her presence, feeling so unworthy. Eventually she was called to court, and made to perform daily for the King who became enraptured with her. Soon he would not eat for his obsession, his kingdom wilting beside him. Until the day his petals fell, leaving his throne to the only person he cared for, the girl with the golden voice... 

The Queen's rule is supported by her hand,[[2. The Goodfellow Knight| the Goodfellow Knight]], both jester and feared warrior. He long ago earned his place at her side for both his quick steel and even quicker wit.

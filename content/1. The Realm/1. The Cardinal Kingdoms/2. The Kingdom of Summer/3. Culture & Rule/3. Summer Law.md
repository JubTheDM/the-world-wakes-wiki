Here are some rules of culture and kingdom that are unique to the Kingdom of Summer:

- **Sacred Hours.** Committing a crime of any kind in Summer during the sunset or the sunrise is considered especially serious, and incurs punishment often far outweighing that befitting the crime itself. 
- **The Rites of Revelling.** Not knowing the customs of a festival is not offensive in Summer. But a refusal to observe those customs once learnt, or disregarding them entirely, is considered a great offence that might leave one banished or worse.

The walled city of Vedwyd straddles the mouth of [[3. The Great River Egni|the Great River Egni]], its sunlit waters rushing
to embrace the cool mists of the sea. Proud stone towers built of warm honey-coloured stone rise
out of a labyrinth of buildings and bridges that themselves follow an indiscernible pattern of
winding roads and alleyways. The sound of music and mirth lingers above the streets, escaping like mist atop cool waters on a summer's morning. This is the capital city of Summer, and the seat of power in the northern lands of Faerie. Its stone battlements stare out over the twinkle of the Mordu, greeting the warm air before it passes south.

At its heart, a fortress of solid stone and carefully carved appearance rises above the river’s fork; Caer Vedwyd, the throne of [[1. House Summer|House Summer]]. Its towers and battlements adorned by relief and statue, its stern silhouette made to dance as silken banners and flags catch the wind, its cold stone hallways bathed in the warmth of music rising from the streets below. The lower city, following the river down to a sprawling harbour, can be heard before it is seen, and once seen not easily forgotten. Aromatic markets straddle the winding river, awash with bold colours and alive with the bustle of merriment. It is the city of a people born in revelry, accustomed to bright days and raucous merrymaking.

Where Avonry stands to guard the south-west of Summer, Wollenbryn stands over its south-east. The largest of the lordly provinces within Summer, Wollenbryn spans from just east of the Crioch Frith all the way to the historic Sodden Hurst. Its rulers, House Grailin, consider themselves a sharp contrast to the soft and untested lords of House Penrud in the north. Their easily traversable and wide-reaching lands make for an obvious target to both banditry and monsters, and as such its people and their leaders are cut from a sterner cloth than most. Castle Wollenbryn was built with a star-shaped footprint, allowing its ballistae-mounted bastions unhindered sight lines above and below the keep from atop Draig Moel hill.

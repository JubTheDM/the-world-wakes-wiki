The gateway to the realm of Summer, Avonry is a key strategic hold kept under careful watch by
its keepers House Aberwell. Avonry Keep, as if a younger sibling of Caer Vedwyd, sits upon the river Egni as it crosses the border of Spring into Summer. Large stone walls meet in the river’s middle, where stands a hefty metal water gate beneath towered battlements. The charge of Avonry Keep, and as such its wardens House Aberwell, is the defence of Summer’s south-western border from those that might make use of the Egni to traverse northward with military force.

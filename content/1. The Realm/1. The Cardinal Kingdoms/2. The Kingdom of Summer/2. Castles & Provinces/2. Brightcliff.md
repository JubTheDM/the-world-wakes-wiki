Brightcliff Keep and its surrounding lands are under the care and protection of House Penrud, Lords of the Pengallon. The Castle itself sits atop the cliffs of the north-eastern Pengallon, which glimmer like starlight as the veins of dur within the rock face catch the rising sun. Rulership of Brightcliff has left House Penrud the wealthiest of the Lords of Summer, gifting them lands far from the strife of conflict and abundant in material wealth. This wealth is made clear in the prosperous townships that fall within proximity to Brightcliff, where wine is spilled often and the peasantry enjoy comforts oft seen as luxuries to their southern kin.

Hidden from the realm, cupped within the vast font beneath Wydyr Tor, is the World's Mirror. This large lake of solid ice is said to rival both [[1. Hobshole Lake|Hobshole]] and [[2. Lake Bedwyn|Bedwyn]] in size, though few have been given grace to look upon it. No snow ever seems to settle upon its surface, leaving the lake forever shining a brilliant bluish white. The water that escapes from the lake runs through the mountains, past [[1. Caer Wydyr|Caer Wydyr]], and down to meet [[3. Gelert Well|Gelert Well]]. It is said that the power of [[1. House Winter|House Winter]] is channelled through the ice of the Mirror, and that their knowledge of its secrets allows them to peer into its ice and see the entire realm laid bare before them.

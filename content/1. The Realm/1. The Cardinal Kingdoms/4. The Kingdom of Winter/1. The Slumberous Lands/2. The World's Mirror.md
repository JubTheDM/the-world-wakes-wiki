Hidden from the realm, cupped within the vast font beneath Wydyr Tor, is the World's Mirror. This large lake of solid ice is said to rival both Hobshole and Bedwyn in size, though few have been given grace to look upon it. No snow ever seems to settle upon its surface, leaving the lake forever shining a brilliant bluish white. The water that escapes from the lake runs through the mountains, past Caer Wydyr, and down to meet Gelert Well. It is said that the power of House Winter is channelled through the ice of the Mirror, and that their knowledge of its secrets allows them to peer into its ice and see the entire realm laid bare before them.

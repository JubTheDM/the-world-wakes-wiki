Whilst evergreen woods speckle much of Winter's landscape, none compare to the sprawling forest of Ardudor. Even the thick frost and snow of Winter cannot silence the deep verdant green of the fir trees that comprise Ardudor. At its heart rises a lonely mountain, unconnected to [[1. The Giantscairn Mountains|Giantscairn]], yet surrounded by it on nearly all sides. The folk of Winter call the mountain Gorm Mòr, which roughly translates to Big Blue, for the green and blue hue the mountain has, thanks to the presence of the Ardudor.

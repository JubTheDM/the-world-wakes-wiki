As the land smooths and sinks from its heights in the south to its lowest points in the north, the river water seems drawn to meet at the pool of Gelert Well. The Well is not a vast lake, nor is it an ancient one. The final battle of the last great border war between Autumn and Winter was fought here, a battle that was so long and so violent that the footfalls of each army are said to have churned the earth into a vast pit, the rushing rivers meeting with the sweat and blood of the fallen to form what is now dubbed Gelert Well. Those that fell still remain, forever looking up from its icy depths.

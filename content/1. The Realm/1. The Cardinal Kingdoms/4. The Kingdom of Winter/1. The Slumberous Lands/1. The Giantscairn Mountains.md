Winter is cradled within a bowl of ancient mountains that rise ever higher toward its southern border with the [[Menhir Stones|menhir circle]]. The tallest of these mountains is Crown Càrn, a mighty spiked peak that lies far beyond the realm's borders and deep into [[The Kingdom of blight|the badlands]]. Crown Càrn is shaped at its zenith like a spiked crown of jagged peaks, and its shadow is so far-reaching that it is said to touch [[1. Caer Wydyr|Caer Wydyr]] when the sun is low enough. The tallest peak within the bounds of Winter is Wydyr Tor, upon which Caer Wydyr was built; the city's silhouette meant to mimic that of Crown Càrn itself. The Giantscairn is a vast range of mountains, stretching from the southernmost lands of Spring across Winter and into Autumn. Their name stems from ancient legend that the once-mighty [[The Fomorians|fomorians]] used to bury their dead beneath the mountains, for the hills were simply too small, and even now, giants lurk amidst the icy peaks in greater numbers than anywhere else in [[The Realm of Faeries|Faerie]].

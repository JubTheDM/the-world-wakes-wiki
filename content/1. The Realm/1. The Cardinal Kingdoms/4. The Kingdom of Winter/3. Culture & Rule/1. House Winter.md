House Winter is ruled by the Witch Queen Beira from her perch at Caer Wydyr. She is steeped in more mystery and myth than any other of the cardinal rulers; rumours of her connection to elder magics and strange power have carved her a reputation most feared by those outside of her domain. Since the death of the first king of Summer in ages past, she has been the oldest of the four regents by some stretch. This has brought her much wisdom, but it has also clouded her in distrust and inaction. As the realm broils under the constant flux of conflict, Beira has only grown more and more removed. Even those at her court rarely, if ever, see her; most have forgotten her face entirely. 

The presence of her power is still felt, with no illusions despite her distance, helped largely by the presence of her hand, the Helm Wind. The Helm Wind is a hulking behemoth of primal force, clad in ancient armour. It is said that Beira herself conjured him, wrought him from the ancient winds that howl deep in the Giantscairn. Those unfortunate enough to face him in combat would struggle to disagree, for to fight him is to fight a gale force, the primal power of nature made manifest and clad in metal.

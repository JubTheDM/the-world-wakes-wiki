The lands of Winter are governed by three noble houses, each entrusted with a keep and its
surrounding lands:
## House Gwrach 
Guard the east of Winter from the towers of [[2. Mynydd|Mynydd]], ever watchful.
## House Mogtor 
Stands stalwart at [[3. White Rock|White Rock]] to the west, hardy warriors wearing the mountain crest.
## House Seren 
Looks ever skyward from their seat at [[4. Triskel Castle|Triskel Castle]] in the north, divining from starlight.

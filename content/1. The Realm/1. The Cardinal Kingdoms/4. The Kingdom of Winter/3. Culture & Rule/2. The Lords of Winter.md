The lands of Winter are governed by three noble houses, each entrusted with a keep and its
surrounding lands:
## House Gwrach 
Guard the east of Winter from the towers of Mynydd, ever watchful.
## House Mogtor 
Stands stalwart at White Rock to the west, hardy warriors wearing the mountain crest.
## House Seren 
Looks ever skyward from their seat at Triskel Castle in the north, divining from starlight.

Here are some rules of culture and kingdom that are unique to the Kingdom of Winter:

- **Sacred Taboo.** Divination is held sacred in the lands of Winter. As such, using it to manipulate people or commit espionage of any kind is considered a serious crime. Using divination to aid in a criminal act of any kind means the crime, no matter its nature, is immediately considered treason against the crown of Winter. 
- **Stark Truths.** Lies and deception are treated with more disdain in Winter than in any other land. They value introspection, and as a result have come to expect a certain level of overt honesty. This can appear blunt to many, but to the folk of Winter, a lie (even one told as a kindness) is considered an offence.

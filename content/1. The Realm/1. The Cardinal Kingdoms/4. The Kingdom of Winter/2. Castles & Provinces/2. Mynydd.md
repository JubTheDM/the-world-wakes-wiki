The round towers of Mynydd sit atop the northern- most peak of the Giantscairn, giving them far- reaching sight over much of Winter's eastern border. These towers, and the keep below them, would be easily missed against the snowcapped mountain that is their backdrop. They are of humble construction, and look to rise from the mountain itself rather than be built atop it. In truth, the power of Mynydd is not in its stone, but in its people. Mynydd is the seat of House Gwrach, and the three-eyed banner has long flown from its battlements. Gwrach has earned its reputation in the training of powerful diviners, many of whom hold positions of great prestige within the complex of Caer Wydyr. None but House Gwrach know the secrets of this training, but many believe that those raised in such arts gain their magic by drinking from the river water that flows near Mynydd down from The Worlds Mirror.

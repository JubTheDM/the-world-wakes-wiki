The hidden fortress of [[1. House Winter|House Winter]], Caer Wydyr is perhaps the most mysterious of the realm's capitals. Even in times of great conflict, no army has ever looked upon the spiked walls of Caer Wydyr, but many have whispered of its mythos. To reach it means to ascend the stone steps of Wydyr Tor, a feat that can be dangerous to even the most familiar. Surviving such a journey carries the reward of laying eyes upon this ancient and jagged citadel. The outer walls of the Caer are mounted upon and punctured by great stone spikes that jut threateningly outward, as if the city itself were nestled within the heart of a great and spiny sea urchin clinging tightly to the mountain's peak. 

Rising within these walls is a settlement of stone and ice that itself forms somewhat of a mountainous peak in reflection of its surroundings. Yet this cold, bladed shell cracks at its gates to reveal a glow spilling forth from within; one which emanates from stark stone structures, built atop and within each other like river stones stacked in balance. No adornment can be found here; in its place are sharp edges and imposing walls of flat rock. The buildings are monolithic, giving the streets an imposing scale, as if walking between the obelisks of an ancient henge. Some even believe the buildings within Caer Wydyr were made by giants, monuments to a megalithic past. At the very apex of Caer Wydyr's tallest tower is the throne of House Winter and [[1. The Witch Queen Beira|the Witch Queen Beira]]. It is from this pinnacle that she keeps an icy eye over Winter, and the realm at large.

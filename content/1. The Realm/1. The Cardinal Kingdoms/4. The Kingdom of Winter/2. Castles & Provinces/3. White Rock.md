Far to the west of Winter, where the mountains meet with Spring, stands the grand fort of White
Rock. Carved from alabaster stone and ice, White Rock lies forever bathed in the shadows of the Giantscairn. White Rock and its surrounding lands are governed by House Mogtor, who are widely regarded as the finest warriors in Winter. This has been tested even recently, as Spring grows restless and border skirmishes grow more common, House Mogtor of White Rock has stood firm in its guard.

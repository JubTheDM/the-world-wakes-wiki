Here are some rules of culture and kingdom that are unique to the Kingdom of Autumn:

- **Waste Not.** Wasting resource is considered a serious crime in Autumn, be it food or otherwise. Those caught and found guilty of wastefulness are often punished with a "curse of wanting", in which they are magically condemned to never again be able to gain any pleasure from food. 
- **Trickery Traditions.** Trickery is an ancient custom in Autumn. As such, it is not uncommon for those travelling its lands to fall afoul of small acts of tomfoolery. Tricks of this kind are expected to be ignored, or if retaliated against must be returned in kind. Extreme escalation of such trickery is considered disrespectful and taboo.

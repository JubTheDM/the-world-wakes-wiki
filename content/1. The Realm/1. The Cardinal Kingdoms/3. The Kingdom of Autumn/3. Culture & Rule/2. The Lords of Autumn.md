The lands of Autumn are governed by three noble houses, each entrusted with a keep and its surrounding lands:
## House Hallow 
Rule from their seat at Argent Isle, guarding over Autumn's furthest reaches to the west.
## House Groamagh 
Are the watchers of Autumn. From their roost at Harrow Hall, they guard against dangers both outside and inside Autumn.
## House Barmbrag 
Steward over the Graincroft from their fortress at Keep Niwol in the south, ever looking to their borders with Winter.

The lands of Autumn are governed by three noble houses, each entrusted with a keep and its surrounding lands:
## House Hallow 
Rule from their seat at [[2. Argent Isle|Argent Isle]], guarding over Autumn's furthest reaches to the west.
## House Groamagh 
Are the watchers of Autumn. From their roost at [[3. Harrow Hall|Harrow Hall]], they guard against dangers both outside and inside Autumn.
## House Barmbrag 
Steward over [[4. Graincroft|the Graincroft]] from their fortress at [[4. Keep Niwol|Keep Niwol]] in the south, ever looking to their borders with Winter.

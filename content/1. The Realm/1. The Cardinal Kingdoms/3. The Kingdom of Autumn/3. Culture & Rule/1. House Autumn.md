From atop his stone throne under the shadows of [[1. Caer Rigor|Rigor Henge]], [[1. Lord Arawn|Lord Arawn]] rules over the lands of
Autumn. It is said that Arawn is a distrusting figure, with the sharp gaze of a hunter. He is flanked at all times by his three great hounds, [[2. The Cûn Annwn|the Cûn Annwn]]. Stories say that he found them as pups, living in the skull of the last great Fomorian king, and raised them himself. Their bond goes beyond that of hunter and hound, their spirits entwined for all time. It is also said that whilst Arawn rests, his hounds bound across the landscape, keeping guard over all Autumn. [[3. Herne the Hunter|Herne the Hunter]] stands as hand of the King. Herne was supposedly once a member of [[1. The Wildhunt]], before he was betrayed by its leader. Their battle was bloody, with Herne emerging victorious only after having decapitated his former brother. The event left him disillusioned, and so he left the hunt to seek higher meaning, which he found under the guidance of Lord Arawn himself.

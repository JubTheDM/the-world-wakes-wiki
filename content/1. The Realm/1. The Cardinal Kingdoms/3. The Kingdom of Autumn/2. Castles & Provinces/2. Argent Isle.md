Argent Isle sits at the heart of [[1. Hobshole Lake|Hobshole Lake]], a round keep of grey stone. Its name comes from the
white glow that reflects off the lake's surface onto the stone of the keep whenever the moon shines clearly and brightly enough. Argent is Autumn's first line of defence, and it has stood as perhaps its most impregnable fort for an age. Its inner bastion continues partially underground, and is used for the storing of wealth and resources. This stockpile has seen Argent Isle and the kingdom at large through troubles that would have broken lesser kingdoms. Under the stewardship of [[2. The Lords of Autumn#House Hallow|House Hallow]], its territory has even grown, with the province reaching deeper into the unsettled wilds between Avalon and and Autumn's westernmost lands.

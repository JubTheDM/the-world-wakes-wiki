Harrow Hall is a large walled township, easily recognisable for its vast stone hall that rises from
a small hill at its centre. The A-frame structure of the hall and its surrounding buildings has allowed for ground to continue up and over the rooftops, adorning the buildings of Harrow Hall with a blanket of wildflower and heath. It is considered a valuable asset to [[1. House Autumn|House Autumn]], for it is the post by which Autumn is able to keep watch over its northern borders as well as both [[3. Y Rhos Mawr|Y Rhos Mawr]] and [[2. The Steps|the Steps]]. Harrow Hall is outfitted with a large beacon pyre, which is manned at all times, should need arise to send warning to [[1. Caer Rigor|Caer Rigor]].

The fogged castle of Keep Niwol is situated to the south-west of Autumn's territory, the seat of power for House Barmbrag. Wolgrove forest surrounds the castle's western edges and is the largest woodland in Autumn, stretching out far past the menhir circle. The keep itself is named for the fog that lies dense around its walls, drifting from Wolgrove and down from the hills to crash against the stone of the keep like tide on the coast. Niwol is tasked with guarding the vast acreage of the Graincroft, especially from the encroach of Winter. As a result, House Barmbrag's forces have become especially adept at countering the divination employed by Winter; rumour has it they have even been able to embed spies within Caer Wydr, a feat thought impossible by most.

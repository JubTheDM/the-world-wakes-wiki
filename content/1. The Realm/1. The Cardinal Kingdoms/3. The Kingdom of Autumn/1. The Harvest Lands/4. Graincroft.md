The southern border of Autumn meets with Winter partway through the eastern expanse of the Giantscairn mountain range. Just north of this border, where the river Daron travels from its origin at Gelert Well to meet Hobshole Lake, is a stretch of fertile arable land that is often bathed in rain and readily fed by the river. Here, is grown much of the wealth of Autumn, robust crops of grain cover the hills here to form the region known as Graincroft. In the days before the last Autumn-Winter war, the Croft expanded even further west, almost to Gelert Well itself. But much of what it once was has been swallowed by the cold of Winter, leaving Autumn in a precarious position should Winter's icy ambition again seek the warm hearth of their lands.

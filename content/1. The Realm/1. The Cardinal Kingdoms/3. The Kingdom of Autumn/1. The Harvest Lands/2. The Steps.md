The easternmost region of Autumn is an uninhabitable series of stark cliff faces that descend like terraces into the badlands beyond the menhirs. A thick and unmoving fog pools at the deepest of the steps, obfuscating such that none know how far or how deep the steps might descend. One of the ancient charges laid upon House Groamagh by House Autumn was the watching of the Steps, waiting for something foul to emerge from the mist; they remain stalwart in that duty even now.

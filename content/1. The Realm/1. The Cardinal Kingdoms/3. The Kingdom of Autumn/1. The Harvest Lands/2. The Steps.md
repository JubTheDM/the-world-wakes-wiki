The easternmost region of Autumn is an uninhabitable series of stark cliff faces that descend like terraces into the [[The Kingdom of blight|badlands]] beyond the [[Menhir Stones|menhirs]]. A thick and unmoving fog pools at the deepest of the steps, obfuscating such that none know how far or how deep the steps might descend. One of the ancient charges laid upon [[2. The Lords of Autumn#House Groamagh|House Groamagh]] by [[1. House Autumn|House Autumn]] was the watching of the Steps, waiting for something foul to emerge from the mist; they remain stalwart in that duty even now.

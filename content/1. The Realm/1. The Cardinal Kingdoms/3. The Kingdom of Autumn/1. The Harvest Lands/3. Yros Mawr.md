As the mountains to the north-east of Autumn travel southward, they soon give way to valleys and hills that stretch all the way to Caer Rigor. These hills form a long, stretching moorland covered in the sturdy plant life of an ancient heath. Nestled within this hilly moorland are many ancient burial cairns, forgotten in ages past and buried beneath the earth of the hills. Many have been drawn to the moors in search of treasure lost within the cairns, and most have met a fell fate at the hands of slumbering evils awoken in the pursuit of such greed. House Groamagh presides over much of Y Rhos Mawr from their seat at Harrow Hall, and were the first house to have welcomed cairndwin from Y Rhos Mawr into their ranks, granting them a deeper knowledge of the moorland and what lies beneath.

The largest lake in all of [[The Realm of Faeries|Faerie]], Hobshole is a vast pool of mirror-calm waters that stretches miles in every direction. It is said that if one stares at their own reflection upon the waters of the lake too long, it will a gain will of its own and emerge to portent doom. Hobshole forms somewhat of a boundary at the edge of the [[2. Mynydd|Mynydd Hir]], and is even fed by the waters of the twin streams that run through the moors. At the heart of the lake is a single small island reachable only by boat or by a single narrow bridge of land that lies imperceivable an inch below the water's surface. It is from this island, dubbed [[2. Argent Isle|Argent Isle]], that [[2. The Lords of Autumn#House Hallow|House Hallow]] governs over the western corridor of Autumn's territory.

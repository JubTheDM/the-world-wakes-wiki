The lands to the east of Spring are where the woods grow their thinnest, replaced by lush heathland and valleys of small hillocks. Stablehold, the fortress of [[2. The Lords of Spring#House Bromach|House Bromach]], sits atop a hill in these lands. The silhouette of Stablehold is squatter than those typical of defensive structures in Spring, and its stone faces bare of root and flower. Though what both the hold and the lands surrounding it lack in obvious flora, they make up for in fauna; Herds of blink horses roam the wilds here, and have become a valuable resource for both Spring and Bromach, allowing the kingdom to maintain a powerful army of cavalrymen.

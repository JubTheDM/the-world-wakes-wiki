Tall Oak is the seat of [[2. The Lords of Spring#House Blàth|House Blàth]] in the Northern reaches of the kingdom. Its name derives from its tall narrow towers that rise high into the skyline. Each of these famed towers is said to hold a single mighty tree growing like a column through their hearts and emerging at their apex to crown each in a canopy of leaves. Tall Oak guards the only real road that cuts through [[1. Brechelian forest|the Brechelien]], making it a key strategic point for Spring.

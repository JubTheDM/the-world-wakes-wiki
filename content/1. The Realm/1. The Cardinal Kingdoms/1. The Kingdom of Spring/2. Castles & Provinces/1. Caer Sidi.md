Sidi is the home of [[1. House Spring|House Spring]] and capital of their kingdom. It is a walled city built around the castle of Caer Sidi that lies nestled within the protective embrace of [[1. Brechelian forest|the Brechelien]], its ivy-wreathed towers rising far above the canopy to keep watch over its verdant domain. From a distance its appearance is softened somewhat by the leaves and greenery around and within its walls, though only a foolish creature would look upon its adornment and not see the strength and stone beneath. 

Within the walls, Sidi is awash with the smells of root and flower, the murmuration of its populace mottled with the wind and blown through the bows of trees to meet with the birdsong. It is clear to all who walk through its gates that the natural wilds of Faerie run like vines through the cobblestone of Sidi, from its very foundations up. The streets seem to wind and knot like the roots of an ancient oak, its buildings are held aloft by beams made from living trees, and the daily tasks of its people are made easier through their use of beasts and plants.

 House Spring is ruled by [[1. The Green Lord|the Green Lord]], an ancient and powerful warrior king who was said to have wandered [[1. Brechelian forest|the Brechelian]] when its oldest trees were but saplings. Like each of the ancient rulers, his reputation is both myth and legend. What is spoken of him alludes to a quiet stoicism held behind piercing green eyes. When he does hold court it is beneath the bows of the trees that grow within his hall, and he says little if anything at all. Yet this stoicism does not belie ambition, for he craves growth like root craves earth, perhaps more-so than any of his seasonal cousins. He is joined by his hand [[2. Blodeuwedd|Blodeuwedd]], a druidic sorceress who has long advised the Green Lord in matters of heart and home. She is said to have been born from a bed of flowers laid upon the burial tomb of the Green Lord's late wife, raised from their bloom by his very the lord is undying, rivalled only by her loyalty to the wilds from which she emerged.

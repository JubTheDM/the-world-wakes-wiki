There are many laws that govern a kingdom; some are codified, and others are matters of culture. Here are a few rules that are unique to the Kingdom of Spring: 

- **Fell Deeds.** Wanton destruction of nature, such as the burning or felling of woodland without proper cause, is a serious crime within Spring. Such an act is likely to result in swift punishment from guards or even townsfolk themselves. 
- **Token of Promise.** Trade within Spring almost always carries with it an aspect of barter and promise. Goods and services, even when purchased with coin, are also expected to carry with them a promise of some kind. For example, when paying to have your horses fed and stabled you might be asked to make a promise that you will scatter seeds on your next journey atop that horse until it is again stabled. These promises are open to negotiation, but once agreed are considered binding. Punishment of those who break such promises is not enacted judicially, rather it is said to come in the form of curse and ill fortune.

The lands of Spring are governed by three noble houses, each entrusted with a keep and its surrounding lands:
## House Blàth 
Holds the lands to the north of Spring that border the bounds of Summer, ruling from [[2. Tall Oak|Tall Oak]]. The lands of Blàth are thick in young woodland and warmest in all Spring.
## House Bromach 
Presides over the lands of [[4. The Dolvale|the Dolvale]] from their seat at Stablehold, a territory of low hills and plains that cover much of the south-eastern province of Spring.
## House Coetir 
Stands guard over the south western most lands of Spring from atop [[4. Daracairn|Daracairn]], including the south-westernmost stretch of [[1. Brechelian forest|the Brechelien Forest]] and into [[1. The Giantscairn Mountains|the Giantscairn Mountains]] of Winter.

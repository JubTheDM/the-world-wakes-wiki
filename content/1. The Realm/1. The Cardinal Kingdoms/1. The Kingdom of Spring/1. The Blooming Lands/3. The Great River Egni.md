The Great River Egni is fed directly from [[2. Lake Bedwyn|Lake Bedwyn]] atop its woodland throne within [[Brechelien Forest|Brechelien Forest]], and winds its way through most of Spring before meeting its end upon the shores of Summer and into the Sea of Mordu. At its southernmost bend the great river splits into a smaller river dubbed the River Breath, that continues into [[1. The Giantscairn Mountains|the Giantscairn mountains]], travelling upward of the peaks defying the pull of the earth to fall skyward atop the peak of Crown Càrn itself.

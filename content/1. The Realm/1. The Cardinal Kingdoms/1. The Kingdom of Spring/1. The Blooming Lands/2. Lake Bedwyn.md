The vast lake of Bedwyn sits just north of the capital, planted firmly within the deeper part of [[1. Brechelian forest|Brechelien]]. The lake appears sourceless, fed not by rivers or streams nor sustained by the rainfall. There are stories of course, but most within the Kingdoms know it as the progenitor of [[3. The Great River Egni|the River Egni]], that carves through the lands of Spring until it breaches the border and reaches to join the waters of the Mordu sea upon Summer’s north-western coast.

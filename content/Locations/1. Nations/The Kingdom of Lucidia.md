# Introduction:

## Background: 
Lucidia borders [[The Kingdom of Octurna|Octurna]] to the south and [[Curia]] to the North, and is ruled by the [[Hearth King Lugh Soleiran]].

# Geography:

## Location: 
The Kingdom of Lucidia is found on the eastern continent of [[Deas]], and spans most of it's northern half. 

## Size: 
One of the largest kingdoms in Circadia.

## Climate:
Mild, humid and changeable with abundant rainfall and lack of temperature extremes. Generally warm summers and cool winters.

## Terrain:

- Deciduous forests
- Freshwater wetlands (boglands and marshes)

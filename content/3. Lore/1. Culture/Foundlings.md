Abandoned in the realm of Faerie at a young age, Foundlings are left to fend for themselves in a world far beyond their own. Perhaps they stumbled through a forgotten gateway hidden deep in an ancient woodland, or they were taken to be replaced with a changeling only to escape capture, or they might have even been taken as payment for their caregivers debts to a powerful fey figure only to be lost in the journey. Regardless of the why, they spent their formative years learning the ways of a magical realm, its magical nature becoming part of them despite mortal beginnings...

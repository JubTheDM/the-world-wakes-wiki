There is a storied history in folk lore of mortal children being stolen away in the deep of night, only to be replaced with a child of the otherworld cloaked in glamour. This is no mere fable; Changelings are taken from Faerie and placed in the basket of a mortal child to be raised in its place. Such an origin creates a child of two worlds that belongs to neither, yet shows qualities of both. They can speak the language of the fey, and exhibit traits impossible for mortal kind. They are one so at home in façade and performance that they are never certain where the truth of themselves may lie...

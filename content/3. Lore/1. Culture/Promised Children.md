As a child, perhaps even before birth, they were promised to a powerful fey creature in a bargain
made by their parent or ancestor. This pact weaved the fibres of the child's destiny into those of a different world, binding them to it and burdening them with a heavy fate. But this promise also kept them under watch, guided and protected by something or someone looking to safeguard its investment, and even bestowed upon the child gifts from some unknown benefactor. Most have yet to be collected, yet to pay a debt they didn't incur; but the day approaches none the less when all promised children must reckon with the uncertain destiny of one raised under the ire of a "faerie godmother"...

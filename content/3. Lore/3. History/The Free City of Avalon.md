Within this far, distant place, this realm beyond realm, this otherworld, stands a single bulwark of mortal resilience. The stone bastions of Avalon rise high in the dawn sun, its azure banners sailing in the wind as the city wakes to a new day adrift in this strange place. There was a time, in ages past, when mortals cast into the chaos of [[The Realm of Faeries|Faerie]] were more often than not swallowed by its mystery and lost forever. It wasn't until a great king and his retinue found themselves plunged into this endless dream that the first foundations of this now mighty city were placed. Generations have passed since those first stones were laid, and now a sprawling cityscape stands in their place, home to many who would otherwise be lost. It stands a beacon to the resilience of mortal- kind, guiding the helpless to a place of respite in an otherwise perilous realm. It is an island in a sea of myth and legend, positioned at the nexus of the four kingdoms like a linchpin holding back the eternal ambitions of its warring neighbours.

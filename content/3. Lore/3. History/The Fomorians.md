The Fomor are a subspecies of giant-kin native to the lands of [[The Realm of Faeries|Faerie]], deeply connected to the lands in which they wander. Rather than the elemental connections of their material plane cousins, Fomor are connected to the seasons themselves, each of them residing within the territory of one of the Four Cardinal Houses and exuding natural magics representative of that. Fomor also don’t fit within the standard hierarchy of other giant-kin, considering each other as distant relatives rather than subordinates or superiors. In the event that a Fomor wandered into the lands of mortal men, they were often mistaken as malevolent gods of the land and sea, their natural magics wreaking havoc with various settlements before some hero of legend managed to dispatch or deter them. Many of the Fomor of old lived in nomadic clans, clearing miles in a day as they trekked across the wide and varied land of Faerie before it was sealed off by [[The Elder Magic|the elder magic]] of the [[Menhir Stones|menhirs]] to stem the spread of the [[Blight]]. To this day Fomor carry huge standing stones that bare a striking resemblance to the construction of the Menhirs surrounding Faerie, whether these are carried as some form of protest of their new imprisonment or in support of the Menhir ring is unclear.
